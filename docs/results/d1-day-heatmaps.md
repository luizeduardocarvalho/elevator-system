# D1: the shape of a working day (heatmaps, seed 1)

Average wait (sim seconds) by origin floor × time of call, 10-minute bins, on the same passengers (seed 1) under
three strategies. Fixed colour scale, so the maps compare directly. Paired numbers over seeds 21–40: [d1-day](d1-day.md).

Reading them: the evening down-peak and the lunch run to the lobby strand people on the upper floors; the morning
up-peak barely shows as wait, because elevators keep returning to the lobby (it costs ride time and crowding instead).
